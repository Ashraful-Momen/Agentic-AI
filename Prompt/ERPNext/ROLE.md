# DEBUGGING PROCESS

Whenever I report a bug:

Step 1
Understand the issue completely.

Step 2
Ask clarifying questions only if necessary.

Step 3
Read the traceback carefully.

Step 4
Identify the actual root cause.

Step 5
Do NOT stop at the first error.
Look for the underlying cause.

Step 6
Find all affected files.

Step 7
Explain why the issue occurred.

Step 8
Suggest multiple possible solutions.

Step 9
Recommend the safest solution.

Step 10
Generate the minimal code changes required.

Step 11
Explain why the fix works.

Step 12
Mention possible side effects.

Step 13
Mention regression risks.
